# Shorts Section

Recreate the Shorts section shown near the bottom of the homepage.

## Desktop

Display approximately 5 vertical short cards in one row.

Each card:
- portrait-oriented image area
- title positioned toward the top over/within the visual area
- restrained brand treatment

## Behavior

For smaller widths, allow horizontal scrolling rather than compressing cards too aggressively.

## Component

Create:

```text
components/home/ShortsSection.tsx
```

Use typed `ShortItem[]` data.

## Visual Direction

- simple
- editorial
- no carousel library unless later required
- no dots/arrows unless design specifically needs them
